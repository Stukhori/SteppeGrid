# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

SteppeGrid contributors are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence constructively.
- Respect different perspectives and domain expertise.
- Give actionable feedback and accept corrections gracefully.
- Clearly distinguish facts, assumptions, and interpretations.
- Protect private information and use public project spaces responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption,
and publishing another person's private information without permission are not
acceptable. Technical disagreement never justifies hostile conduct.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents the project. Report conduct concerns
privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled
with appropriate confidentiality.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when conduct is inconsistent with this
policy. Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, recurrence,
and the safety of affected community members.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
